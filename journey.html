Whitefield AcademyFreshman
The first pole.

October 2020. A school field in Georgia, a borrowed pit, and a father who held a national record in the event. Brenden picked up a pole for the first time that fall and cleared 3.30m before his first meet.

Whitefield AcademySophomore
The first full season.

9'6" at the Bill Thorn Invitational in March. 12'6" at the sectional in May, and a place at the Georgia state meet by the end of the season. Every meet that spring was a first.

  • Bill Thorn Invitational, 2.90m, 1st
  • GHSA 1A Private Sectional, 3.81m
  • GHSA 1A Private State Championships, 4th
Whitefield AcademyJunior
Fifteen feet, a state title, and the Bahamas vest.

Cleared 15 feet for the first time at the Georgia indoor championships in January. Won the Georgia 1A state title in May. In between, flew to Kingston and won his first CARIFTA Games gold for the Bahamas at 16.

  • Georgia Indoor Championships, 4.63m
  • CARIFTA Games, Kingston, gold
  • GHSA State Championships, 4.62m, champion
  • New Balance Nationals Indoor, 10th
Whitefield AcademySenior
The record his father set, broken at home.

Easter weekend, Thomas A. Robinson Stadium, Nassau. 5.06m in front of a home crowd: a CARIFTA Games record, a Bahamas junior record, and the national record his father had held since 1987. A second straight Georgia state title followed, then silver at the NACAC U23 Championships in Costa Rica and bronze at the Pan American U20 Championships.

  • CARIFTA Games, Nassau, gold, 5.06m, Games record and national record
  • GHSA State Championships, champion, second straight
  • NACAC U23 Championships, silver
  • Pan American U20 Championships, bronze
  • Atlanta Track Club Boys Field Athlete of the Year
  • Whitefield Academy Magazine, Higher Heights
Samford UniversityFreshman
The number one freshman in the country.

5.45m at the Samford Bulldog Open in February, a new indoor national record and the best mark by any NCAA freshman that season. A Southern Conference title two weeks later. Then Grenada: a third straight CARIFTA gold and a second Games record. In August, the World U20 Championships in Lima, Peru.

  • Samford Bulldog Open, 5.45m, indoor national record
  • SoCon Indoor Championships, champion
  • CARIFTA Games, Grenada, gold, 5.30m, Games record
  • World Athletics U20 Championships, Lima
  • SoCon Field Athlete of the Month
Samford UniversitySophomore
18 feet. 5.53.

February 7, UAB Vulcan Invitational, Birmingham. 5.53m (18'1¾"), the second-best mark in Samford history and a new national record. A second SoCon title in March. In July he cleared 5.50 at a summer meet, then transferred to Illinois for the Big Ten.

  • UAB Vulcan Invitational, 5.53m, national record
  • SoCon Indoor Championships, champion, second straight
  • SoCon Field Athlete of the Week
  • Transferred to the University of Illinois
University of IllinoisJunior
The Big Ten.

First season at Illinois. 5.45m at the Illini Last Chance meet, fifth-best in program history. 5th at the Big Ten Indoor Championships in Indianapolis, 8th outdoors, and a first NCAA West preliminary round. Two years to Los Angeles.

  • Illini Last Chance, 5.45m, program top five
  • Big Ten Indoor Championships, 5th
  • Big Ten Outdoor Championships, 8th
  • NCAA Division I West First Rounds
Los AngelesThe Games
19 feet, and Los Angeles.

The goal, as his father put it to The Tribune in 2024: qualify for the 2028 Olympic Games and vault 19 feet.
