Pole Vault · The Bahamas
HE BROKE THE
RECORD HIS FATHER
SET IN 1987

Brent Vanderpool set the Bahamas pole vault record at 4.89m in 1987. It stood 36 years. His son broke it at 17, at the CARIFTA Games in Nassau, in front of a home crowd, and has raised it four times since. The current mark is 5.53m, set February 7, 2025.
