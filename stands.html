What he stands for

An athlete is an avenue of a person.It isn't the person.

The vault is the thing he is best at. It is not the whole of him. These are the ideas he actually trains by, in the words he actually uses. Nothing here is a poster.

01 · Health

"I'm not a coach. I'm worried about the health of the athlete to be able to do what you need to do."

He will not talk technique. That is a coach's job. What he shares is the part nobody asks about: the knees, the tendons, the rehab, the boring twenty minutes that let the jumping happen.

02 · Agency

"I'm not trying to tell you how to live. I'm trying to tell you that you have options."

Go along with the program and you move at the rate of the program. He transferred twice, changed countries every Easter, and built his own things off the runway because the option was there.

03 · Reps

"It needs to exist before it can be perfect."

Every bar he has ever cleared, he missed first. He posts once a day, keeps the misses in, and treats the failed attempts as the lesson. Capture the work. Don't manufacture it.
